# Play as Pokemon (experimental)

Turn into any Pokémon in Pokopia. Use their abilities: flying Pokémon can fly, and Grow, Burn, Water and Generate Pokémon can use their ability. Works in co-op / Link Play: every player needs the mod installed.

| Press | Does |
|---|---|
| **ZL + L3** (left stick click) | Turn into the chosen Pokémon; press again to turn back |
| **ZL + D-pad Left / Right** | Pick the previous / next Pokémon (A to Z). While transformed you change as soon as you stop flicking through |
| **ZR** as a flying Pokémon | Take off; tap to rise a little, hold to keep rising faster |
| **ZL** while flying | Descend the same way; keep holding it on the ground to land |
| **ZL + ZR** while flying | Drop out of the air |
| **ZR** (Pokémon that can't fly) or **ZL + D-pad Down** | Use the Pokémon's ability: Grow, Burn, Water or Generate |

This mod replaces **Always Ditto** and **Play as Resident**. Remove those two before using it: they use some of the same buttons. The Pokopia Editor's **Mods** page does that for you when you press Install or Update.

**Only for Pokopia 2.0.0, update 655360** (build ID `76A489F3BD3642FA`). On any other version the patch is not applied.

## Choosing the Pokémon

**While playing (emulator on Windows).** With the mod installed and the game running, open the editor's **Mods** page, pick a Pokémon under **Change Pokémon while playing** and press **Transform**. No restart is needed. **Turn back** returns you to your character. ZL + L3 then uses the Pokémon you picked last, and ZL + D-pad Left / Right go on from it. Until you pick one, ZL + L3 turns you into Ditto.

**A starting Pokémon.** On the Mods page, change the Pokémon list from **Any (choose while playing)** to one Pokémon and press Install. ZL + L3 then turns you into that Pokémon until you pick another with ZL + D-pad Left / Right. Restart the game after changing it. Use this on a console, where the editor can't reach the running game.

**Without the editor.** ZL + D-pad Left / Right pick any Pokémon while playing. The standalone zips start as Ditto. To start as another Pokémon, open the cheat file `76A489F3BD3642FA.txt` in Notepad, find the line `020F0000 03D14C06 00000046`, and replace `00000046` with your Pokémon's code. [README-standalone.md](README-standalone.md#pokémon-codes) lists the code for every Pokémon. Restart the game after changing it.

The files in this folder are the "Any" setup: `76A489F3BD3642FA.any.txt` is the cheat file it installs, and `76A489F3BD3642FA.txt` is the template for a starting Pokémon (written for Piplup). The cheat only sets the Pokémon ZL + L3 turns you into: the buttons work without it (as Ditto). Both, and the patch, are generated by [src/build_emulator.py](src/build_emulator.py).

A Pokémon that doesn't live in your town appears a fraction of a second after the transform, because the game has to load its animations first.

The game also has forms that are not Pokémon: story objects and the shapes Ditto takes for its moves. On the Mods page, pick one under **Experimental forms** and press **Transform** while the game is running. They are only offered there, not as a starting Pokémon, and the button combos keep using the last Pokémon you picked.

## Flying

Pokémon that hover or fly in the game (Charizard, Scyther, Noctowl, Venomoth, Volbeat, Combee, Vespiquen and others) can fly when you are one of them. You get their own hover and flying animations. Climbing and descending handle like a ship: a tap of ZR or ZL moves you a little (about a quarter of a block), holding it speeds up to 4 blocks a second, and letting go slows you down to a hover. Other Pokémon ignore the fly buttons.

## Talking and Link Play

Talking to most Pokémon makes the game turn you back into your character: the conversation needs it. The mod lets it, and turns you back into your Pokémon once the conversation is over and you walk off.

In Link Play every player needs the mod. Then the other players see your Pokémon, walking and moving with its own animations, and you see theirs. Ending a session while transformed leaves everyone's character as it should be (fixed in 1.5). With three or four players a game could crash right after another player changed Pokémon; 1.6 loads a Pokémon's animations before changing anyone into it, which fixes that (details in the [Recompia README](../../recompia/play-as-pokemon/README.md#link-play)).

## Doors, crafting and the bag

Since 1.7 a transformed Pokémon can open doors, use the crafting bench and boxes, open the bag, pick things up, and hold and place blocks, as your character does. Chairs and beds stay for your character: their prompt shows, but A does nothing while you're a Pokémon, because a Pokémon has no sitting animation and would be stuck there. Picked-up items go into the bag without showing in the Pokémon's hand. Tested in Recompia; the emulator version has the same code, untested in an emulator so far. Details in the [Recompia README](../../recompia/play-as-pokemon/README.md#doors-crafting-the-bag-and-building-170).

## Abilities

A transformed Pokémon can use its town specialty: press **ZR**, or **ZL + D-pad Down** (flying Pokémon keep ZR for flying). It plays the Pokémon's own specialty animation and then acts on what is in front of you:

| Ability | Pokémon | Does |
|---|---|---|
| **Grow** | Bulbasaur, Oddish, Snivy, Rowlet, ... (26) | The crops and plants in front of you grow a stage, each once, as when a resident uses Grow |
| **Burn** | Charmander, Vulpix, Torchic, Litwick, ... (34) | The object in front of you is burnt: a campfire lights |
| **Water** | Squirtle, Piplup, Marill, Lapras, ... (37) | Sludge that is not bubbled yet (Bleak Beach) gets Bubbled, with the Bubble animation. Otherwise the Water Gun: it waters soil and plants and washes bubbled sludge away |
| **Generate** | Pikachu, Pawmi, Mareep, Magnemite, ... (27) | The light or machine in front of you is powered |

Each Pokémon uses its own specialty; a Pokémon without one of these does nothing. Burn, Generate and Bubble act as one of the Pokémon living in the area (the game only lets a town Pokémon light a campfire), so a Pokémon must live where you are. Chop, Crush, Recycle and the other specialties are not in: the game does those inside each Pokémon's job routine. Which Pokémon has which specialty comes from the lists on pokopialab.com. Wait until you have finished transforming before you press the button.

## Install

The easiest way is the Pokopia Editor's **Mods** page, which installs it straight into your emulator or saves it in the right folder layout.

Without the editor, download a ready-made zip from the [latest release](https://github.com/cycalo/Pokopia-Editor/releases/latest). Each zip includes a short README ([README-standalone.md](README-standalone.md)) that explains how to install it and change the Pokémon.

- **`z-PlayAsPokemon-emulator-<version>.zip`**: unzip into your emulator's `load` folder
- **`z-PlayAsPokemon-SD-card-<version>.zip`**: unzip onto the root of the SD card

To copy the files by hand instead, use these paths.

### Emulator (Eden, Yuzu and forks)
Copy the files into the emulator's `load` folder like this, then make sure **Play as Pokemon** is ticked in the game's Properties → Add-Ons:

```
load/0400A3A01EE4C000/Play as Pokemon/exefs/76A489F3BD3642FA5E18BD83D32C98BACC5F7D0E.ips
load/0400A3A01EE4C000/Play as Pokemon/cheats/76A489F3BD3642FA.txt
```

Keep **Enable GDB Stub** off, and use the combos only once you're in the world, not on the title screen. After a change, the next one waits about 0.75 s: changing form too fast can crash the game.

### Recompia (PC)
Recompia runs the game natively, so it can't use the IPS and cheat files. Use `recompia_play_as_pokemon-1.7.0.pkmod` instead: copy it into Recompia's `mods` folder and allow it in the launcher's Mods page. It has the same buttons, plus a list of every Pokémon on F3 or ZL + R3. The Pokémon can also be picked in the mod's settings (launcher or F1 in game). See [recompia/play-as-pokemon/README.md](../../recompia/play-as-pokemon/README.md).

### Console with custom firmware (untested)
```
atmosphere/exefs_patches/Play as Pokemon/76A489F3BD3642FA5E18BD83D32C98BACC5F7D0E.ips
atmosphere/contents/0400A3A01EE4C000/cheats/76A489F3BD3642FA.txt
```

This has only been tested on an emulator. A stock console can't load patches or cheats, and modding a console can get it banned from online services.

## How it works
The game identifies every character, including you, by a character number: your human form is 1, Ditto is 70, Piplup is 114. `pokemon.json` lists the number for each Pokémon.

Since 1.5 the patch runs the same code as the Recompia version: [recompia/play-as-pokemon/src/play_as_pokemon.c](../../recompia/play-as-pokemon/src/play_as_pokemon.c), compiled for the Switch's CPU by [src/build_emulator.py](src/build_emulator.py), with [src/switch/switch_host.c](src/switch/switch_host.c) doing inside the game what Recompia's mod loader does on the PC (memory, calls into the game, the controller through the game's own `nn::hid` functions, the clock). So both versions behave the same and get the same fixes.

- The code goes into two functions of the game that nothing calls or points to (debug drawing code, at `main+0x6B32C0` and `main+0x6B8BA0`); the mod's variables into game memory nothing uses (`main+0x3D15000`).
- At each of the mod's eight hook points (the same game instructions as the Recompia mod's `mod.toml`) the patch puts a jump to a small trampoline that saves the registers, runs the mod's handler and then either carries on with the game's own instruction or returns from the game's function.
- The buttons are read from the controller every frame (handheld, or controller 1), so combos and flying react at once.
- What the handlers do: while you're transformed the player's "desired form" reports your Pokémon and requests to turn back are ignored; the "something is in the way" check that starts Ditto's stand-up stretch passes; the Pokémon's animations are loaded (the game only loads those of Pokémon living in your town) and the form is built again once they are ready; flying Pokémon get the game's own hover motion and a climbing speed; abilities play the Pokémon's specialty animation and then call the effect on the cell in front of you; talking turns you back for the conversation; in Link Play the other players' Pokémon are shown the same way; and the game's world reset (the end of a Link Play session) makes the mod forget the animations it had loaded.

The cheat file only writes the Pokémon ZL + L3 turns you into (`main+0x3D14C06`). The editor's live controls write it too, and the form held (`main+0x3D14C00`). Neither touches your save.
