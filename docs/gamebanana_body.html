Pokopia Editor

Edit your Pokopia save and install game mods. One exe, nothing to install. For Pokopia 2.0.x on Eden, Yuzu, Suyu, Sudachi, Citron, Torzu, Ryujinx and Recompia.

Getting started

  1. Download PokopiaEditor.exe.
  2. Close your emulator.
  3. Double-click the exe. It finds your emulator or Recompia save on its own and backs it up before every save.

If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway (the app is unsigned).

What you can do

  • Overview: rename your player, see your stats, max friendship, learn every emote, unlock the wardrobe in one click
  • Bag: drag in any item, swap slots, set amounts
  • Item Catalog: search every item, including Expansion Pass and event items
  • Residents: set friendship and find their homes on the map
  • Moves & Emotes: learn or unlearn, set up your wheels
  • Wardrobe: unlock any outfit or hairstyle
  • Collection: complete the Pokédex, Habitat Dex, recipes, records and more
  • Island Map: edit your islands. Move, place and delete objects, paint paths, raise, lower or flatten the ground, and build any of 252 habitats in one click
  • Photos: export your in-game album at full size
  • Backups: make, compare and restore backups
  • Quests (experimental): see where every quest stands and finish one that is stuck
  • Advanced: every story flag with a description

New in 1.9.2: Play as Pokemon 1.7.1 (doors, crafting, the bag and building as a Pokémon, swimming and diving, headbutting fruit trees) and the Quests page.

Mods

Install them from the editor's Mods page, straight into your emulator or onto an SD card.

  • Play as Pokemon: Turn into any Pokémon in Pokopia. Use their abilities: flying Pokémon can fly, and Grow, Burn, Water and Generate Pokémon can use their ability. Works in co-op / Link Play: every player needs the mod installed.
    ZL + L3 transform · ZL + D-pad Left / Right pick a Pokémon · ZR / ZL fly or dive · ZR or ZL + D-pad Down ability · A doors, crafting, the bag
  • Unlimited Crafting Storage Range: crafting uses every storage box you have placed, at any distance.

Without the editor or for Recompia, grab the standalone zips and .pkmod files from the GitHub releases.

Notes

  • Mods are for Pokopia 2.0.0 (update 655360) on emulators, and 2.0.1 on Recompia.
  • Your live save is only written when you press Save, and a backup is made first.
